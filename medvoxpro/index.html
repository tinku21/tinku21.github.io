How MedVox pro Works

A seamless, continuous workflow from the exam room to the finalized record.

01
Record

The physician opens the app, selects a patient, chooses a format, and begins dictating. If dictation is not your thing, you can also enter the notes manually.

02
Transcribe

Audio is processed into text with formatting, coding extraction, and confidence analysis.

03
Generate Note

The transcript is structured into the selected clinical format using AI.

04
Extract Codes

Extract ICD10, CPT, SNOMED, and CPT2 codes from the notes automatically. Edit and lock after proofing.

05
Review Flags

Uncertain words are reviewed, confirmed, or edited by the physician.

06
Professional Review

Transcriptionists and Coders can verify accuracy, refine, and lock the notes before exporting.

07
Export and Done

Finalized notes can be exported to integrated downstream systems like EHRs, or emailed/shared through third party secure apps.
